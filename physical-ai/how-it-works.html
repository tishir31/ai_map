How the atlas works

Every research and workspace view is available without sign-in. Start with Today, follow the evidence, and save your decisions.

Your daily workflow

  1. Today: review a bounded queue of people, companies, investors, and market changes. Save notes, watch, dismiss, or mark contacted.
  2. Research & Lineage: explore papers, centers, people, companies, maps, collections, timelines, traces, comparisons, and sourced entity dossiers.
  3. Landscape, Companies, Capital, and Activity: inspect the market from different perspectives and export the relevant data.
  4. Open Workbench for company research, link analysis, verification, Data Tables, Research Sheet, Review Queue, and Ingestion Health.

What is saved

Edits, review approvals, exclusions, tags, map layout, and Today decisions are personal work saved in this browser. Review approval updates your working copy. Use Workbench → Export workspace backup to keep or transfer that work. Restore replaces the saved workspace on the destination browser.

The shared published dataset loads from the server. Reloading published evidence preserves your personal edits. Reset workspace edits removes your market-data overlay and reloads the current published dataset. It does not erase Today notes or tags.

Research runs

The Research Sheet starts with published market coverage. Start run submits a public research prompt and queues source research. Prompts and sourced results are public; use public information only. The worker checks every six hours. The run badge shows queued, running, or completed state; a queued run is not a completed investigation. Company research and Analyze link return a candidate for immediate review.

Evidence and interpretation

Published market events link to public citations. Graph identities use stable entity keys, sourced relationships, and explicit dates. Graph snapshot dates and market-event dates are separate. Heuristic company scores and generated memos are research prompts, not verified facts. Participated deal value is not an investor's check size. Unknown research-table fields stay blank.

Public operation

Browsing, research, and personal workspace tools require no login. Generation has shared usage limits. Private mailbox text and server credentials are not returned to the browser. Health shows aggregate pipeline status. On the parent map, Build public digest downloads approved evidence; newsletter insights are a dated publication archive.
