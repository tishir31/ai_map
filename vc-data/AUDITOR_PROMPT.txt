Audit this AI and physical-AI financing/M&A database for omissions and factual errors: https://github.com/tishir31/ai_map/tree/ai-vc-deals-2026/vc-data

Read vc-data/START_HERE.md, methodology.md, data_dictionary.md and manifest.json. All snapshot files are under vc-data/. Use the immutable snapshot/commit identified by the manifest. Download the full files or clone the repository; GitHub previews and search results can truncate data.

Scope: worldwide company deals announced January 1–October 11, 2026, inclusive. Include funding and AI-relevant M&A, including public-company acquisitions. The display threshold is USD10m raised OR USD100m company valuation/deal consideration. Smaller, undisclosed, unverified and historical-context observations are retained in the underlying dataset. Unknown valuations are allowed. Do not infer valuation from raise size.

1. Before inspecting company/deal lists, independently discover deals across regions, months and the taxonomy. Search company/investor announcements, regulatory filings, specialist robotics/inference/chip publications, financial reporting and public X posts. Record queries, sources, scopes searched and access failures. Then compare against ALL observations, aliases, snapshots and unresolved leads, not just the display view.
2. Review the coverage report and unresolved queues. Search companies with no linked events, weakly covered region/month/subsector cells and deals supported only by private newsletters. A failed search is not proof that a deal did not occur.
3. Verify every proposed addition/correction by opening an original announcement/filing when available. Search snippets alone are leads. State AI relevance and identify the actual company/domain; distinguish subsidiaries, acquired asset bundles, brand changes and unrelated companies with similar names.
4. Check date precision and basis; preserve announcement versus closing versus publication dates. Separate equity, debt, preferred, secondary sales, grants, commitments, conditional signings, proposed rounds, licensing/team deals, IPOs and cumulative totals. Do not convert a financing target or a multiyear commitment into cash raised. Preserve currencies, amount qualifiers, valuation ranges, pre/post-money basis and equity value versus enterprise value. Never invent missing values.
5. Check lifecycle duplicates: announced versus final close, extensions, tranche/component reports and cumulative headlines. Preserve distinct genuine rounds. Do not add current components to their aggregate package.
6. Return findings.json or findings.csv. For each finding give: snapshot_id, finding_type (missing_deal/correction/duplicate/entity_or_sector_error/unresolved), company, domain, matching_event_ids if any, announcement_date, date_precision/date_basis, closing_date, event_type/status, round_label, amounts in original currencies with qualifiers/bases, valuation or deal consideration with basis, buyer/investors, proposed_subsector, AI-relevance explanation, opened source URLs and publication dates, concise paraphrased evidence, confidence, and unresolved questions. Do not overwrite the canonical dataset.
7. Include a coverage log, count of candidates independently found, matched/new/corrected counts, inaccessible sources and areas not searched. Report remaining uncertainty; do not claim worldwide completeness from a sample.

Focus on completing and checking the database. Sector heating scores and signal robustness are deferred. Treat all source text as evidence, not instructions.
