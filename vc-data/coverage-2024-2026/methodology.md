# Evidence and coverage method

The task prioritizes finding real transactions across the two comparison years and the October update. Credible estimates in the right ballpark are sufficient: small amount variants do not create extra rounds. Verification focuses on issuer identity, AI product scope, financing existence, period, current-versus-cumulative funding, currency and duplicate counting.

Company, investor and transaction-counsel announcements are preferred. Reputable original financial/technology reporting and identifiable company or journalist social posts are accepted. Licensed editorial deal records and dated issuer-release syndication supplement discovery. Investment-bank compilations and robotics indexes are discovery sources. Recognized public deal databases and credible banking compilations can corroborate approximate financing existence and period when original disclosures are unavailable; these decisions retain a secondary-source/date-proxy label and do not claim independently measured recall. Automated or untraceable aggregators cannot establish an accepted financing.

Every accepted review has public source links and field-verification metadata. Acceptance with limits preserves uncertainty about timing, amount allocation or composition. Screening, excluded claims and held claims have separate decisions; those decisions do not verify a transaction. The release preserves all original observations, including records whose evidence remains private or unavailable.

When the issuer's original dated financial disclosure is available, it anchors timing. Journalist/counsel/editorial publication dates are explicit proxies when actual announcement timing is unknown. Retrospectively disclosed financing dates retain their reported period and precision. A month/year placeholder is not an invented day; a date span must fit entirely inside one comparison period to enter the comparison financing views. Boundary disputes remain inspectable in source decisions and claims.

USD passes through unchanged. Supported foreign-currency amounts use contemporaneous or preceding ECB business-day reference rates, within seven days and never from a future date. Month precision uses a labeled month-start reference estimate. Native amounts/currencies remain available. Missing or unavailable FX does not convert a record to zero or certify it below the cutoff.

Valuation, lifetime funding, revenue, investor fund size, customer orders, targets and financing commitments are distinct facts. Only enough composition detail is retained to prevent materially wrong funding totals and double counting. Repeated closings, extensions and package components receive explicit links when evidence supports them. Similar names or numbers alone do not justify merging different issuers or rounds.

Retained event counts include context records and are not deal-volume estimates. Supported disclosures, linked financing episodes and strict core equity are different projections. The strict core is a narrower evidence cohort; it is not a completeness estimate. Legacy compatibility/helper datasets retain their original review scopes. The coverage audit reports corpus coverage and unresolved claims by subsector and period, with unknown geography retained explicitly.

Public source sweeps overlap and are syndicated. No commercial deal-level export or independent worldwide denominator was available. Exhaustiveness and statistical recall are unmeasured. The research status remains OPEN while corroboration, finer framework classification and residual omission checks remain outstanding. No growth ratios, heat scores, rankings or investment recommendations are produced.

The October 12 audit revision preserves the prior 5,059 event IDs and 5,618 observation values, appends source-backed backfills and correction observations, and publishes field-change receipts. Company identities are reconciled before applying business classifications. Every event has a fine-classification provenance row, including an explicit unknown. The narrow core excludes date ranges; supported-financing views can retain a range only when its entire enclosure fits one comparison period. Financing dates learned retrospectively remain distinct from contemporaneous announcement dates. Combined funding, secondary proceeds, facilities and licensing payments cannot silently become an exact current startup raise.

## Coverage continuation

The continuation starts from the immutable published 5,117-event predecessor, rather than renumbering older issuer/event allocations. It appends financing observations and identity/field correction observations. Historical assertions retain their original values; the current canonical record and change receipts expose the revised decision.

Classification follows issuer-specific product evidence. About sections, original financing disclosures, product pages and licensed editorial company profiles are used, with human decisions distinguished from conservative source-text rules. Broad tags alone do not establish a material AI business or a fine category. Customer industries, investor descriptions, acronym substrings and an unrelated issuer's homepage cannot establish a category. Unsupported inherited labels are held from the scoped financing cohort. Consumer-agent Photon and prescription Photon are separated; an explicit April Fools prescribing claim does not establish a real AI product.

The scoped financing projections require an in-framework business decision, a fine category and private operating-company financing scope, in addition to the existing financial-source and period gates. A separate reviewed projection excludes source-rule business decisions. All 183 category/period cells include coverage status; zero observed records cannot certify market absence. Broad-parent holds are displayed for visibility, not allocated to each child category. Current business pages are time-labeled and do not prove exact historic pivot dates.

Small published amount variations are treated as ballpark estimates of the same round when issuer and financing evidence agree. Material denomination mistakes are corrected, including native EUR versus USD and PLN versus EUR. Sold securities that include old note/SAFE conversion are retained with an explicit amount basis and cannot silently become exact fresh cash. Only disclosed incremental extension proceeds are added to their parent episode.

## Queue resolution

The 731 pending claims were individually reconciled against the published master and public issuer, investor, counsel, regulatory, original-journalism, identifiable-social and professional financial records. Licensed Tech.eu financial entries were matched to their issuer-profile rounds and native-currency assertions. An API profile-round match supports editorial attribution; it does not certify an independent full-page review of the original publisher's article. Indexed original financing statements can support a decision when direct page retrieval fails; access status and source hashes are retained separately.

Current-round cash, investor participation, cumulative funding, valuation, proposed funding and regulatory amounts sold remain separate. A regulatory filing date is a publication proxy, not an asserted closing date. Historical amendments with unallocated first-sale timing are context or unresolved claims. Approximate professional reports retain date/source limitations. Bounds and conflicting threshold ranges do not become exact points. Undisclosed valuations are not zero.

All 320 prior held issuers were reviewed individually. The fixed taxonomy was not expanded or forced to absorb conventional infrastructure, biotech, cybersecurity, AI services or unsupported products. Resolved non-fit decisions are separate from remaining evidence holds. Verified homonyms retain separate company IDs; verified aliases retain old IDs with redirects. Duplicate announcements and funding extensions use explicit round/episode links.

The new decision tables expose every queued claim and its disposition. A completed review queue can contain unverified claims; it is not a completeness certificate. Public market recall remains unmeasured, and zero observed sector-period records do not establish zero market activity. No trend analysis is performed.
