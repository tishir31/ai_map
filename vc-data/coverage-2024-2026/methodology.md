Factual record policy

Keep the financed company, announcement or public-disclosure timing, reported amount and currency, round where stated, source URL and exact supporting text. Preserve original observations and identify duplicate disclosures. Retain claims without public corroboration with an explicit uncertainty status.

Credible company/investor announcements and professional reporting establish facts. Discovery snippets and attached links are checked against the issuer and specific round. Unknown amounts remain null. Reported lower/upper bounds retain their qualifier. Public-source disagreement remains visible rather than becoming an invented precise allocation.

The user controls business-framework and instrument exclusions. No source is admitted merely to clear a heat-map rule. No deal-count calculations, trends, period growth, scores or rankings are produced in this release. October2026 remains a dated update to the raw records.
