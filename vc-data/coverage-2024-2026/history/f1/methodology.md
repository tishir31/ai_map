# Methodology

Current snapshot `ai-deals-coverage-f1aefd5696e41b8783b3`, built from f306 at commit `f430906f94206519c9fd08cead427ee482da6640`. Original observations are immutable; canonical corrections append observations and field-level source receipts. The predecessor is reproducible from the pinned Git commit. Current receipts are never historical receipts relabeled as fresh verification.

Comparison periods are October 1, 2024–September 30, 2025 and October 1, 2025–September 30, 2026. October 1–11, 2026 is a display update. A disclosed date range must fit wholly inside one period. Month/week proxies retain their precision. First public disclosure assigns an equity episode to its period; later extensions/closings do not create a second episode. Cross-period aggregate proceeds stay unknown.

Use `heatmap_equity_episodes` for financing counts. An announced or publicly reported completed equity/convertible round counts once, supported by admissible public evidence and an in-framework business classification. Pure debt, commitments, fund capital, subsidiary/JV capitalizations and talks are excluded. Confirmed equity in a mixed package counts once; its equity amount stays unknown unless disclosed separately. Primary/secondary packages and combined rounds keep their reported context but do not masquerade as a single known equity-cash amount. Unknown amounts remain null, not zero. Bounds remain lower/upper bounds. No missing amounts are estimated.

Native currencies remain preserved. Existing foreign-currency conversions use contemporaneous or preceding ECB business-day rates, within seven days, with dated FX provenance. Month precision carries its existing explicit FX date basis. USD conversions are arithmetic representations of source-stated native amounts, not a new estimate of missing proceeds. A stated acquisition price in another currency is retained in that currency; no undocumented USD conversion is invented.

The changed-fact ledger contains exact short quote fragments and public URLs. The private source cache was checked against every fragment. The Information, Axios Pro/newsletters, Dealroom News, user CSVs and low-quality aggregators are discovery leads, not sole counted-record evidence. Reliable public journalism, company/investor press releases and regulatory documents are admissible. A row marked unverified is excluded; a completed decision is not a fact-verification certificate.

Acquisitions use one record per verified buyer/target deal, dated by announcement rather than closing. Private startups are the main bucket; public companies and carve-outs are context; license-and-hire deals form a separate bucket. Price status distinguishes disclosed, reported and undisclosed. Basis, stake, consideration form and closing date are explicitly unknown when sources do not establish them. Stake consideration is not silently grossed up to a full-company valuation. The public acquisition view is authoritative; legacy raw M&A records are retained leads and observations.

All 61 fine categories and 32 original parents use the same minimum of ten equity episodes in each 12-month period. `comparison_ready` means this sample-size rule only. It is not comprehensive source coverage. Qualified fine groups plus residual small-child parent groups form a nonoverlapping partition. Empty observed cells are not certified zero-market cells. Taxonomy gaps, issuer uncertainty and historical/current product timing remain explicit.

The digital search freezes six source families across the same 24 months, plus 104 weekly AlleyWatch URLs. Mention extraction and source availability are published. Fixed test company/date units were excluded from in-sample diagnostics; publisher families still overlap. The supplied 145-row test remains fixed. Targeted repairs are reported, so current test capture is a post-repair diagnostic rather than an untouched blind holdout. Historical broader-financing capture is re-evaluated under H01 and never quoted as current equity recall.

The acquisition sweep freezes 55 buyers and one corporate-press/SEC search query per buyer in each comparison period. This establishes identical search depth. It does not certify that every corporate release or every SEC filing was enumerated.

Historical review tables and old receipts are retained for lineage, not current admissibility or current readiness. The old broader inventories and legacy helpers do not replace H01. Private source payloads, mail identifiers and local paths are excluded from public data. Portable standard-library validation reconstructs SQLite from verified manifest JSON/CSV chunks and re-runs current checks. No trends, growth rates, scores or rankings are computed.
