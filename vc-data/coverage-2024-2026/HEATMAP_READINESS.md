## Permitted use and limits

Use the seven qualifying subsectors for a clearly labeled, bounded public-financing comparison. Suppress comparisons for the other 54 subsectors, including neoclouds; their recorded financings can still be inspected. The frozen reference selection has strong robotics coverage, many US large rounds and some European/China/India coverage. It does not adequately test every digital/engineering subsector or provide an exhaustive country-specific market denominator. A 100% repaired-reference capture rate is not 100% worldwide completeness.

Use linked financing episodes for deal counts, assigned to the first public disclosure period. Use dated incremental disclosures for funding amounts; do not add the same episode total again. Reported packages and unknown composition stay marked. Missing amounts and upper/lower bounds must not be silently converted to zeros or exact cash. Older source-rule classifications are explicit and sampled rather than all recertified individually.

Twenty old discovery claims remain quarantined. They are unverified assertions, not proven omitted deals. There are zero unresolved source-supported period-financing classification holds. Those quarantined assertions do not keep this completed bounded assessment open.
