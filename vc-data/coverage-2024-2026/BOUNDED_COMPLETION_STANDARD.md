# Bounded heat-map readiness assessment

Frozen before new external benchmark reconciliation, 13 October 2026.

Scope: the fixed 61 subsectors; 1 October 2024–30 September 2025, 1 October 2025–30 September 2026, and the 1–11 October 2026 update. Assess public financing announcements. Do not calculate growth, heat scores, rankings or investment recommendations.

## Completion of this assessment

1. Freeze the published predecessor and preserve every existing event and original observation. Retain corrections with source provenance.
2. Reconcile a finite external reference set of at least 200 separately identified financing announcements. Include at least 60 in each 12-month period and at least 8 in the October update. Select references from original reporting, issuer/investor announcements and independent sector/region funding lists, without selecting only companies already in the database. Record source selection, eligibility, duplicate links and both initial and final capture. This measures reference-set capture, not worldwide market recall.
3. Review all 183 subsector/period cells, all 24 comparison months, sparse cells and geographic source coverage. Assign an explicit readiness status rather than interpreting an empty cell as zero financing.
4. Independently check at least 60 existing financing records, stratified across periods, subsector families and individually reviewed versus source-rule classifications. Fix every material error found and extend the same correction to an affected issuer or clearly defined shared error pattern.
5. Correct every credible in-scope omission discovered in this finite assessment, at any amount. Treat a missing financing of at least US$10 million (the existing cutoff), a wrong period/issuer/currency, a duplicate episode, or a misclassification as material. In sparse cells, any omitted financing is material to deal counts. Preserve uncertain amounts as approximate or unknown; do not spend effort reconciling immaterial amount disagreements.
6. Give every reference and finding a final disposition. Unverified discovery claims remain quarantined and are not counted as confirmed missing deals. Do not require proving that every rumor never happened.
7. Run canonical, public-export and public-only reconstruction checks; regenerate the inspection CSV, manifests and provenance; publish and verify commit-pinned remote files.
8. Publish one final verdict and a finite list of any specific exclusions or unresolved material constraints. The assessment ends after these steps, even if the justified verdict is not ready.

## Readiness rules

- **Ready:** all mandatory data-integrity checks pass; no verified material finding remains unfixed; eligible reference-set capture is at least 95% by count overall and in each comparison period, and all eligible reference financings at or above US$10 million are captured; all 61 subsectors have adequate independent support in both comparison periods.
- **Ready with specific exclusions:** the same integrity, repair and reference-capture requirements pass, but some subsectors/periods do not have adequate independent support or have systematic scope/collection uncertainty. Publish exact excluded cells and permitted uses. Do not describe excluded cells as cold or zero-activity sectors.
- **Not ready:** material errors remain, required checks fail, or the assessment does not establish enough comparable coverage to support the requested heat map even with explicit exclusions.

For this bounded assessment, adequate independent support means at least three eligible externally referenced financing episodes in each 12-month cell, corroborated across at least two independent publishing/source families for the subsector across the comparison window, plus no unresolved systematic classification or collection defect. Sparse cells may contain accurate records but fail this coverage threshold. October is a short update, not a third 12-month comparison; its verified records can be displayed, while absence of announcements is not evidence of no activity.

Thresholds are practical review rules selected before reconciliation, not statistical guarantees. No unqualified claim of worldwide completeness or measured market recall will be made.

## Finite work sequence

Freeze predecessor and standard; select and preserve external reference sources; reconcile reference announcements; review classification/period/duplicate samples and weak cells; apply the finite correction set; calculate coverage diagnostics only; validate; publish; issue the verdict.
