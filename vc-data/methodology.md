# Methodology and limits

Scope is worldwide AI, physical AI and enabling infrastructure financing and AI-relevant M&A announced during the stated 2026 window. Public-company acquisitions are eligible. Display thresholds are USD10m raised OR USD100m valuation/deal consideration. The underlying corpus retains smaller, undisclosed, historical, disputed and prospective observations. Missing valuation is permitted; missing does not mean zero.

Company financing, issuer valuation and acquisition consideration are separate fields. Current-round equity, debt, secondary liquidity, commitments, cumulative funding and financing targets remain distinct. A multi-year credit facility or financing cap is not cash raised. Private preferred, warrants, license/team arrangements and unknown instruments remain explicitly flagged. Directory financial snapshots are context and may be estimates or cumulative amounts; they are not transaction events or proof of native transaction currency.

An announced round and verified bank-account receipt are different claims. `raised_amount` retains the source-supported current financing amount when disclosed; publication does not independently verify receipt of every dollar. `equity_round_announcements` counts supported financing announcements even when cash is unknown, a signed commitment exists, or composition is mixed. Use `core_equity_all` for the stricter current pure-equity cohort, and apply amount basis, qualifiers and null handling before summing reported cash. Signed/forthcoming financing commitments belong in `committed_amount`, with `raised_amount` left unknown unless funded proceeds are disclosed.

Financial context categories are caveats, not extra monetary quantities. `capital_commitment_disclosed` requires a structured non-null commitment field; `financing_target_not_closed_raise`, `cumulative_or_combined_round_total` and `mixed_financing_package` derive from financial basis/composition. Other finite categories identify source-reported timing, valuation-basis, instrument and perimeter limits. An investor named Committed Capital, commercial GPU capacity, or a previously committed component inside a newly announced total does not by itself mark that entire round as a new future commitment.

Public IPO and listed-placement context preserves gross versus net proceeds, expected proceeds, base shares versus greenshoe shares, offer price versus traded price, and signing versus closing/listing dates. These fields never become private VC raised cash. A proposed placement remains proposed even when its maximum expected proceeds are known.

Announcement, publication proxy and closing dates have independent precision/basis fields. Month-only records may contain a first-day storage sentinel; use the precision and month, never the sentinel as an exact date. An announcement in 2026 may disclose cash raised in 2025. Rumours, negotiations, signed agreements and completed transactions remain separate statuses.

All retained source observations survive in `observations_all`; assertions may disagree. Canonical event values reflect current reconciliation, not elimination of conflicting evidence. Lifecycle groups identify related announcements, updates, tranches and final closes. Counting views prefer one record per lifecycle and preserve distinct true rounds. Review flags, original currencies, amount bounds and valuation/consideration bases remain visible. Unknown instrument, aggregate-only funding, unresolved price/date conflicts and blocked evidence limit analytical eligibility. A record with a primary web source does not mean every field is confirmed by that source: valuation may be separately newsletter-reported. Whole-company enterprise value is not consideration for an initial minority stake; the independent amount/basis fields and derived caveat categories preserve this distinction.

Evidence came from connected newsletters, the uploaded research/export, Physical AI Atlas, company and investor announcements, regulatory filings, reporting and public social sources. Coverage is uneven across regions, languages, subsectors and months. Indexed searches and selective article reads are separately logged; pagination completeness describes the stated source query, not worldwide discovery. A negative search finding is bounded to sources checked and is not proof that a deal never occurred. Registry classifications and headquarters may remain provisional.

Country availability is measured separately from country normalization. A null ISO can mean a missing raw label, explicit multiple headquarters, or a known label without one recognized ISO. Source-backed country assertions are exported only with a valid public evidence URL. Assertion company IDs use the current snapshot. References from the bounded country audit use the named prior published snapshot namespace; original registry-gap IDs are a different historical namespace and are never presented as current company IDs.

The public export uses an explicit allowlist. Email URLs, message IDs, sender addresses, source keys, email subjects, excerpts, raw notes, raw amount claims and original JSON are withheld. Public monetary fields, dates, statuses, publisher attribution and nontracking original URLs remain. Audit text is converted to finite paraphrased issue categories, and free-form commitment basis is converted to a financial category. Public source URLs do not imply that the entire article was accessible. Public and private datasets should have identical retained-observation, event, company, lead and snapshot row counts.

Do not infer sector heat from this snapshot alone. Independent discovery, calendar coverage, alias/ownership review, denominator checks and verification of pending cases are required before interpreting funding speed or valuation trends.
